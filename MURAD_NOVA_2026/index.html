03 / OUR STORY
BUILT WITH HEART

FROM ZERO.
WITH LOVE.

The Murad House began in 2018 with a simple dream: create a place where people feel at home through coffee.

Through difficult times, uncertainty, and war, the dream kept going. With faith, resilience, and the spirit of Lebanon, we kept pouring, welcoming, and believing.

This is just the beginning. One day, inshallah, we hope to share that feeling in many more places.
